# Ajaia Docs

## Product purpose
A lightweight collaborative document editor for internal teams: write a doc, format it, share it
with the right people at the right permission level, and never lose the original.

## Users
Teammates at Ajaia drafting and reviewing shared work: planning notes, specs, meeting docs. They
work on laptops at a desk and check shared docs on phones between meetings. Reviewers of this
take-home are the first users: they will switch between three demo accounts to test sharing.

## Tone
Calm, quiet, competent. The tool disappears into the writing. Copy is short and plain; it states
what happened and what to do next.

## Anti-references
- Google Docs clone (blue toolbar, Material chrome).
- Notion-style warm cream with emoji everywhere.
- SaaS dashboard tropes: hero metrics, gradient accents, identical card grids.

## Principles
1. The document is the hero. Chrome stays thin and recedes while writing.
2. Permission is always visible: you can tell at a glance whether a doc is yours, and whether you
   can edit it.
3. Never lose work silently: save state is always shown, and conflicts are explained.
4. Works on a phone, not just survives on one.
